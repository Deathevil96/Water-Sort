# Privacy Policy — Palette Pour: Sort Puzzle

Last updated: 8 November 2026
Developer: Misal Interactive (misalinteractive96@gmail.com)

Palette Pour is an offline puzzle game.

**Data we collect:** none. The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

**Network access:** this version of the game does not request internet permission and never connects to any server. All art, fonts, levels and sounds are bundled inside the app.

**On your device:** your progress, settings, gold and lives are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

**Advertising, analytics and purchases:** this version contains none. If a future version adds ads, analytics or in-app purchases, this policy and the Play Store "Data safety" section will be updated first, and the third-party providers' own policies will apply to what they collect.

**Children:** the game contains no personal-data collection and no links out of the app.
