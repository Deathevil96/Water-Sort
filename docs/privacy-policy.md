# Privacy Policy — Palette Pour: Sort Puzzle

Last updated: 25 November 2026
Developer: Misal Interactive (misalinteractive96@gmail.com)

Palette Pour is a free puzzle game paid for by ads. This policy explains what data the game and its partners collect.

## What we collect ourselves
Nothing on our own servers. We do not run any server, ask for your name, email or account, or sell personal information.

Your progress, settings, gold and lives are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Third-party services in the game
The game uses two Google services. They collect data under their own policies:

**Google AdMob (ads).** Shows banner, full-screen and optional rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate rough location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Ads are used to pay for the game and to measure and prevent fraud.

**Google Analytics for Firebase (analytics).** Tells us how the game is played, for example which levels are started, won or failed and when ads are shown, so we can fix problems and balance difficulty. It may collect an app instance ID, the advertising ID, device model, OS version, country (from IP address) and in-game events. It does not get your name or contacts.

Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Your choices
- **Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland):** when you first open the game you are asked whether ads may be personalised and data may be used. You can change this at any time from the privacy option in the game's Settings.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset it or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

## Delete your data
- **On your phone:** uninstall Palette Pour. This deletes all progress, settings, gold and lives straight away.
- **Analytics data held by Google:** email misalinteractive96@gmail.com with the subject "Palette Pour data deletion". Say which phone (model) and roughly when you played. We will ask Google Analytics to delete the data for your app instance within 30 days and reply when it is done. No data is kept after deletion.
- **Ad data:** AdMob data is controlled by Google. Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings; Google's own controls are at https://myadcenter.google.com.

## Data security and retention
Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit. Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in Google Analytics (at most 14 months) and is then deleted.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes
If we change this policy we will update this page and the date above.
